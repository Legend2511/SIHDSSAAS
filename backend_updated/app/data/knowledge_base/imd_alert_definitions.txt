# IMD Alert Level Definitions

## Red Alert — Take Action
A Red Alert is the highest warning level issued by the India Meteorological Department. It indicates extremely severe weather conditions are expected that pose a significant threat to life and property.

When a Red Alert is active:
- Rainfall may exceed 204 mm in 24 hours
- Wind speeds may exceed 170 km/h
- Temperatures may exceed 47°C (extreme heatwave) or drop below -10°C (extreme cold wave)
- Authorities should activate emergency response plans immediately
- People should stay indoors and avoid all non-essential travel
- Evacuations may be ordered in vulnerable areas
- All outdoor activities and events should be cancelled
- Emergency shelters should be opened and stocked

## Orange Alert — Be Prepared
An Orange Alert indicates significantly bad weather expected. Conditions are unusual and potentially dangerous.

When an Orange Alert is active:
- Rainfall may reach 115-204 mm in 24 hours
- Wind speeds may reach 100-170 km/h
- Temperatures may reach 44-47°C (severe heatwave) or drop to -5 to -10°C (severe cold)
- Be prepared for disruption to daily activities
- Secure loose objects, outdoor furniture, and equipment
- Stock essential supplies (water, food, medicines, flashlights)
- Keep emergency contact numbers handy
- Monitor official updates frequently
- Schools and offices may be directed to close
- Fishing operations should be suspended in affected coastal areas

## Yellow Alert — Be Aware
A Yellow Alert means adverse weather is possible over the next few days. Conditions could change for the worse.

When a Yellow Alert is active:
- Rainfall may reach 64-115 mm in 24 hours
- Wind speeds may reach 60-100 km/h
- Temperatures may reach 40-44°C (heatwave) or drop to 0 to -5°C (cold wave)
- Stay updated with the latest weather forecast
- Plan outdoor activities carefully
- Farmers should take precautionary measures for crops and livestock
- Fishermen should exercise caution near the coast
- Keep rain gear and protective clothing ready
- Be aware of the possibility of waterlogging in low-lying areas

## Green — No Alert
No significant weather warning. Normal weather conditions prevail. Continue with routine activities while staying aware of regular weather updates.
